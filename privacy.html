Privacy notice

Prt Circle (Pty) Ltd ("PRT CIRCLE") respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

This website

This website has no forms and sets no tracking cookies of its own. Videos are played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we use your name, number, email and message only to reply and to quote for work. We do not sell or share it.

Client work

When we build workflows that touch your clients' personal information, we act as your operator under POPIA. We sign a written operator agreement before access, use only the access the task needs, and return or delete information when the work ends.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email admin@prtcircle.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
