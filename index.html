Questions

Straight answers.

We already use Xero, Dext or Hubdoc. Why would we need you?

Keep them. Xero's own AI now handles more inside Xero, like bank reconciliation. We automate the tasks between your tools that those apps do not cover, such as a client's spreadsheet, a booking system or a CRM feeding Xero.

Do you have client case studies?

Not yet. We are a young company and every video on this page is a demonstration build on test data. That is why the first five clients get a founding price, a 50% balance only due when it works, and a refund if it does not.

Can you show it working before we pay?

Yes. We show a live demo and review your task for free. The build itself is paid, with half due only once it works on your real file.

What happens if a tool changes and the workflow breaks?

You get 14 days of fixes after handover. After that, the optional care plan covers monitoring and repairs. Workflows are built to flag failures, not fail silently.

Who owns the workflow?

You do. Where possible it runs in your own accounts, and you receive a plain guide on how it works.
