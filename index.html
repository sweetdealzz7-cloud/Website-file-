FAQ

A few things worth clarifying.

Does it work with Property Finder, Bayut and Dubizzle?

The system is configured around the inquiry format available from your portal and inbox workflow, including all three.

What happens if an inquiry format changes?

Parsing and extraction rules are tested and maintained against your actual inbound formats.

Does it automatically contact buyers?

No. The system alerts the responsible broker. Human contact stays entirely under your brokerage's control.
